[Full changelog](https://github.com/kubevela/kubevela/compare/v1.9.9...v1.9.10)

## Summary

**Don't use this release.** After upgrading to it, updates to Applications deployed with v1.9.9 or earlier hang, and the Application reports `resourcetracker ... exists but controlled by another application` ([#6493](https://github.com/kubevela/kubevela/issues/6493)). Use [v1.9.11](./v1.9.11.md) or later.

## Bug fixes

- zstd encoder concurrency fits the container's CPU limit; `kubevela/pkg` v1.9.0 ([#6476](https://github.com/kubevela/kubevela/pull/6476)).
- A service port can differ from the container port ([#6477](https://github.com/kubevela/kubevela/pull/6477)).

## Known issues

- Existing Applications cannot be updated after the upgrade ([#6493](https://github.com/kubevela/kubevela/issues/6493)). Fixed in [v1.9.11](./v1.9.11.md) by a `kubevela/pkg` bump ([#6507](https://github.com/kubevela/kubevela/pull/6507)).
