## Summary

**Don't use this release.** It has a fatal client bug ([#6493](https://github.com/kubevela/kubevela/issues/6493)). Use [v1.9.11](./v1.9.11.md) or later.

## Bug fixes

- zstd encoder concurrency fits the container's CPU limit; `kubevela/pkg` v1.9.0 ([#6476](https://github.com/kubevela/kubevela/pull/6476)).
- A service port can differ from the container port ([#6477](https://github.com/kubevela/kubevela/pull/6477)).

## Known issues

- Fatal client bug [#6493](https://github.com/kubevela/kubevela/issues/6493). Fixed in [v1.9.11](./v1.9.11.md).
