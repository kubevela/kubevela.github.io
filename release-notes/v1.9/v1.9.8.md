## Summary

Adds labels and annotations to the `gateway` trait and sets the namespace on the chart's ServiceAccount and RBAC objects.

## New features

- The `gateway` trait supports labels and annotations ([#6397](https://github.com/kubevela/kubevela/pull/6397)).

## Bug fixes

- The chart sets `metadata.namespace` on the controller's ServiceAccount, Roles and RoleBindings, so `helm template` output applies to the release namespace ([#6423](https://github.com/kubevela/kubevela/pull/6423)).
