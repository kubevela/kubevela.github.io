## Summary

Adds labels and annotations to the `gateway` trait and fixes the Helm template namespace.

## New features

- The `gateway` trait supports labels and annotations ([#6397](https://github.com/kubevela/kubevela/pull/6397)).

## Bug fixes

- Helm templates set the namespace ([#6423](https://github.com/kubevela/kubevela/pull/6423)).
- `GetFakeClient` doesn't look up a kube client ([#6428](https://github.com/kubevela/kubevela/pull/6428)).
