## Summary

Moves the addon registry to `kubevela.github.io` and fixes a `vela top` crash.

## Behaviour changes

- The addon registry uses `kubevela.github.io` and follows redirects ([#6133](https://github.com/kubevela/kubevela/pull/6133), [#6147](https://github.com/kubevela/kubevela/pull/6147)).

## Bug fixes

- `vela top` no longer crashes when viewing an Application topology ([#6127](https://github.com/kubevela/kubevela/pull/6127)).
