## Summary

Fixes the `hpa` trait and `cron-task`, and loads local ComponentDefinitions recursively.

## Bug fixes

- The `hpa` trait's memory metric now uses `mem.type` and `mem.value`; before, it used the `cpu` values ([#6434](https://github.com/kubevela/kubevela/pull/6434)).
- `cron-task` supports annotations, resources and volume mounts ([#6422](https://github.com/kubevela/kubevela/pull/6422)).
- Local ComponentDefinitions load recursively ([#6414](https://github.com/kubevela/kubevela/pull/6414)).
