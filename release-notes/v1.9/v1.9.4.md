## Summary

Updates the workflow engine to v0.6.0 and adds the `container-ports` trait.

## New features

- `container-ports` trait for `hostPort` and `hostIP` ([#6187](https://github.com/kubevela/kubevela/pull/6187)).

## Bug fixes

- The arm64 image builds again ([#6170](https://github.com/kubevela/kubevela/pull/6170)).
- `deploy` is a built-in step, so it is not parsed in offline mode ([#6201](https://github.com/kubevela/kubevela/pull/6201)).
